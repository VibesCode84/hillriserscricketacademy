# HillRisers — brand rules

Brand assets and guidelines are in `/brand` (`brand/GUIDELINES.md`, production files in `brand/svg|pdf|png`, originals in `brand/reference`). The website's marks live in `src/components/Logo.tsx` (paths in `src/components/logo-paths.ts`).

- Full brand name: HillRisers. Colours: navy #071B3F / #0A2A5E, gold #E8B84E, ivory #F5F0E6.
- Logo: shield + simplified Harrow spire + rolling hills. No cross, no church/castle building, no extra scenery.
- Horizontal logo for desktop headers; icon-only crest for mobile and favicons.
- Flat colours for embroidery and production SVGs. Don't invent new logo variants; preserve proportions. Simplify rather than add detail.
